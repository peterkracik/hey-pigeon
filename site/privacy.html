Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Hey Pigeon runs on your device and talks directly to your Google account. It has two optional features that use a small relay server, described below; neither ever receives your email. The developer does not see your email, contacts, or account data. For those two features the app sends the relay a Google access token for your mailbox, to show who is asking: the relay checks it with Google, never uses it to read mail, and does not store it.

What data Hey Pigeon accesses

When you connect a Google Account, Hey Pigeon requests the following scopes, used only to provide the corresponding feature:

  • gmail.modify — read your mail and change labels (archive, mark read/unread, trash).
  • gmail.send — send mail on your behalf when you compose or reply.
  • userinfo.profile — your name and avatar, shown in the app.
  • contacts.readonly / contacts.other.readonly — autocomplete when composing.
  • drive.appdata — a hidden, per-app folder in your Drive (invisible in the Drive UI, readable only by Hey Pigeon) used to sync reminders across your own devices.

Where your data lives

All Gmail data Hey Pigeon reads is stored in a local database on your device only. It is never transmitted to any server operated by the developer. The relay described below never receives mail. OAuth tokens are stored in your operating system's secure keychain, never in plain text.

The relay (optional)

Push notifications on iPhone and iPad go through a relay that sees your email address and a change counter and stores a one-way hash of the address with your device's push token. To register, the app sends the relay your Google access token, which the relay checks with Google and does not store.

Read status is off unless you turn it on for an account. When it is on, each message you send from that account contains an invisible image, and the relay stores a random identifier for that message, a one-way hash of your address, and the time of each open (or that the message reached a device that hides opens), for 30 days. It sees the IP address and mail app of whoever loads the image while answering the request. The relay's code does not store or log them, and its request logs are discarded, not stored. The app sends the relay your Google access token, checked with Google and not stored, to register a message and to ask what was opened. If push notifications are on as well, the relay sends your iPhone or iPad a silent push through Apple when one of your messages is opened, so the app asks sooner; that push says only that something was opened, not which message or by whom.

Turning read status off stops it on that device at once: nothing more is added to mail sent from it, and it asks the relay to delete the account's records, right away if it is online and otherwise when it next syncs. Your other devices follow when they next sync; until then a device that has not heard may still send a message with the image. Removing the account from a device asks the relay for the same deletion, which also clears read status for mail already sent from your other devices. If the account is removed or signed out before the relay could be reached, its records are left to expire within 30 days. What a device has already recorded about opened mail (the status in the message and the Recent Opens list, which shows the last 90 days) stays on that device until the account is removed from it; the relay's own 30-day limit does not shorten it. Once it is off on a device and that request has been answered, the device does not contact the relay about that account. Tracking opens can require the recipient's consent under EU and UK law; you are responsible for that when you turn it on.

AI features (optional)

If you enable an AI feature (e.g. summarize, draft), Hey Pigeon sends only the specific content needed for that action directly from your device to the AI provider you configure (OpenAI first), using an API key you provide and control. The developer never sees this traffic or holds your API key. Use of that provider is subject to its own privacy policy and terms.

Limited Use disclosure

Hey Pigeon's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

No selling, no ads, no analytics

Hey Pigeon does not sell or share your data, does not show ads, does not run any analytics and does not track you. Read status, if you turn it on, tells you when mail you sent was opened (see above).

Revoking access & deleting data

You can disconnect an account at any time in Hey Pigeon's settings, or revoke access directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Since your mail is stored only on your device, uninstalling the app or deleting its local data folder removes it. The app asks the relay to delete its records when you turn off push or read status or remove the account, and read-status records expire on their own after 30 days.
