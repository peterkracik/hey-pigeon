The idea

Done, later, or waiting on you.

Mark done, Remind me, Need reply: three things to do with an email, and little else. Flat rows, no avatars, no folder tree in view. The actions live in gestures on the phone and in keys on the Mac, and they are called the same thing on both.

Swipe, and it’s done.

A long swipe right on iPhone marks an email done: the row slides out and an Undo toast takes its place. No confirmation dialogs. Each direction has two tiers, and you choose what sits on them.

Remind me.

Send an email away until later today, tomorrow or next week, or only if nobody replies. Reminders sync between your devices through a hidden folder in your own Google Drive.

Keyboard-first on the Mac.

Single letters for everything you do to mail, ⌘K for everything else, and ? for the whole map.

E Mark done H Remind me N Need reply S Pin JK Next, previous / Search

Local-first, so nothing waits.

Your mailbox is kept in a database on your device. Opening, searching and marking done happen there at once, and Gmail catches up in the background. Offline, what you do is kept and sent when the network is back.

All your Gmail accounts.

One inbox across accounts, each with its own colour, or one account at a time.

Need reply.

Flag the mail someone is waiting on: N on the Mac, a long swipe left on iPhone. It gets its own tab with a count, and a widget, so what you owe an answer never sinks into the inbox.

Bundles.

With AI on, mail about one thing (a trip, an order, a project) is gathered into a bundle with a summary, tracking links and key facts.

Part of the system.

Widgets for Unread, Next reminder and Need reply. Siri and Shortcuts. Your mail in Spotlight. A share extension, and a menu bar extra on the Mac. Liquid Glass only where something floats over your mail.

Careful with what mail brings.

Messages render with scripts off. Remote images stay blocked until you load them, and tracking pixels are removed and counted.

AI

AI on your terms. Or not at all.

The inbox is complete without it. When you want summaries, reply drafts, rewrites, “Ask my inbox”, bundles or suggestions, you choose who answers.

Without AI

Mark done, Remind me, Need reply, search and the keyboard map need no model. Auto-summaries, suggestions for new mail and bundles each have their own switch.

On your device

Apple Intelligence on the iPhone, iPad or Mac itself: no key, no account, and nothing leaves the device. It can suggest Need reply or a reminder for new mail; nothing changes until you accept.

A bigger model, by choice

Opt in to Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, or paste your own key for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. The text a feature needs goes from your device straight to the provider you chose.

No server for your mail. Ever.

Your mail never goes to a server of ours. Hey Pigeon talks straight to your own Google account: Gmail for mail, a hidden Drive folder for reminder sync, and keeps a local database on your device. If you use an AI feature, the text it needs goes from your device to the AI provider you chose, and nowhere else. Two optional features use a small relay that never receives mail: push notifications on iPhone and iPad, and read status, which is off unless you turn it on and then tells you when mail you sent was opened. The privacy policy says exactly what each one stores.
